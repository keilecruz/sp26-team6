# GlowUp

## Title

GlowUp - Book Beauty & Hairstyling Pros You Can Trust

## Team Members

- Keile Cruz-Maldonado
- Maisha Fyruz

## Description

GlowUp is a mobile/web application that allows customers to explore, discover, book, and review trusted beauty and grooming professionals such as hairstylists, barbers, makeup artists, nail/lash artists, and other professional care providers. The app allows professionals to showcase their services, availability, pricing, and sample work, while customers can browse verified profiles, book appointments, and leave reviews based on their experience. This application supports two user roles, customers and providers, with different functionalities to help services be managed clearly and reliably.

## App Functions

### 1. Customer

1. **Create/modify customer profile** — Allow customers to register an account and manage personal information.
2. **View available services** — Browse provider profiles, services, prices, reviews, and availability.
3. **Book available services** — Book an appointment with a selected provider.
4. **Write reviews for booked services** — Leave ratings and written reviews for providers.

### 2. Provider

1. **Manage provider profile** — Create and update the professional business profile, including business name, bio, profile image, and sample work gallery.
2. **Manage services** — Add, edit, and delete offered services with descriptions and pricing.
3. **Manage availability** — Create or remove available time slots for customer booking.
4. **View customer reviews and ratings** — See customer reviews connected to the provider profile and view the average rating based on submitted reviews.
5. **View and confirm bookings** — View customer appointment bookings, including selected service, availability, customer name, and phone number, then confirm pending bookings.
